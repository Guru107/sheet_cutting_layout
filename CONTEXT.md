# Sheet Cutting Layout

This context describes how sheet-cutting plans become manufacturing outputs for press parts.

## Language

**Sheet Cutting Layout**:
A controlled plan for cutting one raw-material sheet into finished press parts and residual material.

**Shearing BOM**:
A bill of materials for the shearing operation, whether created from a Sheet Cutting Layout or created directly.

**Process Scrap**:
Material separated as loss during the shearing process, independently of any End Piece.

**End Piece**:
The residual sheet segment left after the planned parts or strips are cut.

**Disposition**:
The classification of an End Piece as either Scrap or Reuse.

**Scrap**:
Output material without an intended reuse in the cutting plan.

**Reuse**:
An End Piece disposition indicating that the residual material remains available as usable output.
